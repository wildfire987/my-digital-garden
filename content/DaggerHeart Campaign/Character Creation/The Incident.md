It was supposed to be a pretty standard vault raid, that's why the Adventurer's Guild sent in one of the newer parties, the **Fortune Finders.** The dungeon was scouted ahead and reported back to the guild, and it looked like a standard level C or D dungeon, a network of ancient, half-submerged sea caves carved into the jagged cliffs of the XXXXX Coast.

While the rest of the group stayed on the 2nd floor to loot the few treasure chests and skeletal remains, myself, [[Pluton (Deceased) |Pluton]], and [[Dust]] went down to the 3rd floor. This floor was different, far from the cramped confines of the floors above. The 3rd floor had massive 50 foot tall vaulted ceilings that were definitely NOT natural. On this floor we encountered way more treasure and artifacts than we had ever seen before. This was the first time I had ever seen [[Dust]] move quickly, he's normally very slow and calculated in all things. They were all over the room examining tomes and casting minor magic spells on all the different artifacts on this floor. I didn't know what he was looking at, but it was obviously important. *I picked up a VERY HIGH level explosive magic spell when I first got onto the 3rd floor, which I I was planning on giving to [[Hulda]], when they came down.*

*[[Pluton (Deceased) |Pluton]] was the one who accidentally set the trap off*. The ancient, load-bearing magical runes of the cave system flared a violent, angry red. Within seconds, a series of grinding gears echoed through chamber, and the ceiling began to collapse, threatening to bury us alive or drown us as the ocean water rapidly surged in. We only had a few moments before we would all be crushed to death. I looked to see what [[Dust]] was doing as they're usually the ones to get us out of a jam like this. But they didn't even seem phased by what was going on. They just kept examining a damn book like they had all the time in the world.

 [[Pluton (Deceased)|Pluton]] yelled that he thought he found a possible emergency escape tunnel, but it was getting blocked by falling rocks. He was holding back more rocks from collapsing the tunnel as I screamed for the others to come down!

I tried to wait for [[Hulda]] to come down, she would use a spell to blast open the escape tunnel and we'd be free. But right as the rest of the party came sprinting down the stairs to the massive 3rd floor, the entire ceiling started to collapse and come crashing down to crush all of us. I had picked up a VERY HIGH level explosive magic spell when I first got onto the 3rd floor, which I I was planning on giving to [[Hulda]].

Time seemed to stand still in that moment. I was looking from Pluton holding open the escape tunnel. To Dust still looking at a book as casually as you would at a library. To the rest of Fortune Finders on the other side of the chamber. To the rocks hurtling towards our heads to crush every one of us. 

*I fired off the high level explosive magic spell I found to try and blast open an a way out near the escape tunnel. I've never been one to use magic. It's not really my thing. So it surprised me more than anyone how large and powerful the spell ended up being. The shock wave didn’t just open a tunnel; it shattered the structural integrity of the entire cavern. The blast tore through the cave with horrifying force. There was nothing left of [[Pluton (Deceased)|Pluton]]. The most skilled scouts could only find a single ring of his left behind at the scene among the rubble in the following days.* The only solace I can take is that he probably didn't even know what happened.

I didn't even have time to scream his name before the secondary concussive blast of my own magic threw me backward into the churning, rising vortex of the black ocean water. Shrapnel tore through my armor, my ribs shattered, and as the cave completely collapsed behind my escaping friends, I sank into the dark, freezing deep, bleeding out and losing consciousness. I should have died there, swallowed by the sea. But as my vision faded into black, a strange pressure wrapped around my torso.

It wasn’t the crushing weight of rocks, but the soft, incredibly strong grip of a bioluminescent reef octopus. Displaying a bizarre, uncanny intelligence, the creature didn't try to eat me; instead, it used its eight powerful arms to anchor my broken body, dragging me through a labyrinth of narrow, submerged stone tunnels that led away from the collapse. It brought me to a hidden, air-filled sea alcove high above the tide line. For days, as I lay feverish and broken, the creature stayed by my side, keeping my wounds clear of debris and gently pressing damp kelp against my parched lips until my body began to heal.  I was able to hunt for larger fish and the land animals trapped in the network of rubble, and Reginald was able to find underwater plants I could dry out to be able to burn and use as a fire. We forged a silent, unbreakable bond in that dark cave as we were both trapped waiting for help.

*When I was finally found by another adventuring party weeks later, I was taken, with the my new octopus friend Reginald, back to the Adventurers Guild HQ. I didn't receive a hero's welcome. The Adventurers Guild tribunal was already waiting. The trial was short, they weren't looking for facts, they were looking for a scapegoat.* [[Pluton (Deceased)|Pluton]] was the son of a high ranking Merchants Guild member. He was supposed to take over his fathers position of power within the Merchants Guild. But when [[Martine]] was drafted to the Adventurer's Guild, Pluton joined him. He wasn't going to let his best friend go out there alone. [[Pluton (Deceased)|Pluton]] was only supposed to be in the Adventure's Guild until he got "Being a hero out of his system..." That's why he joined a low stakes group like the Fortune Finders. *And [[Dust]]... No remains of him were found, physical, magical, or otherwise.*

The verdict was swift and merciless: my adventurer's badge was snapped in half, and I was expelled from the registry. "Under no circumstance should you have used a Xth level spell, it was irresponsible!" We lost 2 members of the party, a politically important son of a powerful merchant, and the oldest member of the Adventurer's Guild! *I didn't even get a chance to tell my side of the story.*

In the months after I've met with all the members of the Fortune's Finders club. Some of them sympathize with me. Others very much blame me for what happened. Apparently [[Hulda]] was almost finished casting a much more precise and focused blast that would have opened an escape tunnel AND kept everyone alive. **I should have trusted my team instead of trying to play the hero.**

But I'm not one to take things laying down. "**Where the Path Ends, We Begin.**" It's the slogan of the Adventurers Guild, and what keeps me going. 

If the Adventurer's Guild won't listen to my story, I'll make them hear it.

There's a funny little rule in the Adventurers Guild. If you discover a new uncharted territory... The discoverer gets to "claim" representation of it for the Union. Like planting a flag in undiscovered land. Once you do this you gain permanent representation within the Union just like other high level members, along with all the other benefits like priority privileges during meetings. 

But there's another rule, once a town, or community grows large enough it should by right have representation within the Guild. And the person or person's who helped build up that town would be voted in by the townsfolk to represent the new city within the Adventurers Guild.

**That's my plan, build up the city of XXXXX, bring prosperity to this frontier town, and gain it's representation within the Union to be able share my side of the story. One mistake should not define me.**
