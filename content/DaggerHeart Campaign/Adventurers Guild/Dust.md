Tall imposing figure that wears a thick black cloak and and gloves. You never really get to see their appearance typically. But during our adventuring we do discover that they are a Infernis, but completely a skeleton. Typically the only thing you can see beneath the hooded cloak is glowing eyes

They are *immortal*, and centuries old. Very little is known of their back story or why they joined the adventurers guild. They typically do not talk very much, but are adept wizard who is many times the only reason that our guild makes it out of situations alive.

After the Incident:
No remains of him were found, physical, magical, or otherwise. He's not been seen since.
