The Adventurer's Guild is a guild with hundreds of members across the lands, and is made up of smaller groups within the guild. Each group typically consists of 5-10 members. My specific club within the guild is called the Fortune Finders

- **Hulda**
	- Petite, fair-skinned goblin girl with crimson eyes and short dark brown hair, wearing a dramatic wide-brimmed witch's hat and a purely decorative eye patch.
	- She is a hyper-theatrical arch-wizard obsessed with "Explosion" magic, stubbornly refusing to learn any other spell despite the fact that casting it typically leads to unintended consequences. Her wizardly pride often masks a deeply loyal friend who just wants to see shit blow up.
	
- **Pluton**
	- Human boy who is described as "roguishly handsome," swaggering, and deeply expressive. He has a charismatic presence and carries himself with the quiet confidence and stealthy grace of a classic fantasy rogue.
	- Pluton was a guaranteed shoe-in for a safe, comfortable life in the wealthy Merchants Guild, keeping with his family's long-standing tradition. However, when his lifelong best friend Martine was unexpectedly drafted into the Adventurers Guild, Pluton's fierce loyalty took over. In a spontaneous act of defiance against the system, he volunteered to join the Adventurers right alongside Martine to ensure his friend wouldn't face the monsters outside the walls alone.
- **Martine**
	- Martine is the only half-elf living in the human-dominated city of ?????. His most defining-and to him, frustrating-physical traits are his pointed ears, which clearly give away his mixed heritage and make him a target for local bullies, causing him to constantly try to hide them. Physically, he is a bit of an anxious pushover who prefers sweets to physical training.
	- Martine never wanted to be an adventurer. He desperately hoped to join the prestigious Mages Guild so he could master his innate magical talents and make enough money to pull his single human mother out of the impoverished servant class. However, the Mages passed him over, but he was explicitly drafted into the Adventurers Guild instead.
- **The Twins**
	- Twin human females that are impossible to tell the difference between. Physical changes to one of the twins happens to the other twin so that they always look exactly identical. Until they open their mouth.
	- **Soleill**
		- They are a druid hear who is extremely kind and whom my character quickly had a crush on. She is the only one who still semi-regularly corresponds with me after I was forced to leave the guild.
	- **Lune**
		- They are a loudmouth Bard. I cannot stand them. They know how much I like Soleill and one of their favorite things to do is try and trick me to make me think that they're Soleill so they can play pranks on me. Their fiercely loyal to the group, but you have to put up with their antics because it's "all in good fun"
- **Dust**
	- Tall imposing figure that wears a thick black cloak and and gloves. You never really get to see their appearance typically. But during our adventuring we do discover that they are a Infernis, but completely a skeleton. Typically the only thing you can see beneath the hooded cloak is glowing eyes
	- They are *immortal*, and centuries old. Very little is known of their back story or why they joined the adventurers guild. They typically do not talk very much, but are adept wizard who is many times the only reason that our guild makes it out of situations alive.
